# ai-docs — AI Engineer, QA, SecOps & Analysts
- Document Clean Architecture boundaries, contracts, assumptions, threat models, and operator/user workflows in concise, reusable artifacts.
- Map security requirements to OWASP Top 10 risks and testable abuse cases; document tenant isolation, least privilege, secrets handling, and recovery.
- Break large designs into bounded micro-prompts with inputs, owner, scope, dependencies, deliverables, acceptance criteria, and verification evidence.
- Keep test plans reproducible and distinguish planned checks from executed checks. Do not claim a result without evidence.
- Record every work item in `osc_docs/notes/YYYYMMDD-XXX.md` using local date and next three-digit sequence. Include objective, scope/decisions, outcome, and verification (`not run` if not performed). Never include secrets or sensitive personal data.
